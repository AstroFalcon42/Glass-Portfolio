Klein Bottle

2026 · Handblown glass · Salusa Glassworks, Prescott AZ

A Klein bottle — a one-sided surface with no boundary, topologically impossible to construct in true three dimensions. This clear glass interpretation makes the self-intersecting form tangible and holdable.
