Spun Glass Ship

2026 · Handblown glass · Salusa Glassworks, Prescott AZ

A fully rigged sailing vessel built entirely from clear glass. The hull, mast, spars, and standing rigging are constructed from spun and drawn glass rods, mounted on a textured glass base.
