Anglerfish

2026 · Handblown glass · Salusa Glassworks, Prescott AZ

A deep-sea anglerfish rendered in handblown glass. The body is formed in dark amber and brown with white glass teeth and fins, topped with a signature bioluminescent lure in amber and yellow. Suspended mid-swim.
